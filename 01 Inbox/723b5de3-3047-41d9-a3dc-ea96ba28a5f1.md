Welcome to your first **Area**. An Area in Exocortex is a long-running
responsibility — something you maintain, not something you finish (that's
a Project). Examples: "Health", "Finances", "Side projects".

This sample Area contains one Project ([[00d0631d-56c7-4e4b-b98a-fc0b908e6bc9|Build my first project]]) which in
turn contains two Tasks. Open any of them to see how Exocortex layouts
render the parent/child hierarchy and the action buttons.

Try it:

1. Click on [[00d0631d-56c7-4e4b-b98a-fc0b908e6bc9|Build my first project]] in the Asset Relations panel below.
2. From that Project, create a new Task via the "Create child Task" button.
3. Mark [[ebabd2e2-491f-48a1-85db-6b5e63e16a9a|Read the docs]] as Done and see the status pill update.
